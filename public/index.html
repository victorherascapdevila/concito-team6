FOOD FOOTPRINT

The big CLIMATE DATABASE was made for CONCITO by 2-0 LCA in February 2021 and was awarded the Nordic Council Environment Prize.

The newest version (V1.2) from 2024 includes life cycle GHG emissions for 540 food products in the market for Denmark (DK), Great Britain (GB), France (FR), Spain (ES), and the Netherlands (NL).

The DK version is updated with money from the prize, whereas the GB version is funded by Zedible, and the FR, ES, and NL versions are funded by 2-0 LCA.
